Key Statistical Benefits
• Power Boost: If your text features regular text 90% of the time (\(\pi_0 = 0.90\)), the adaptive threshold dynamically expands the discovery area by dividing the standard critical slope boundary by \(0.90\) (gaining roughly an 11% increase in statistical power).
• Fewer False Clean Drops: This modification prevents the testing layout from becoming overly conservative when analyzing large textual corpora, catching marginal true anomalies that classic Benjamini-Hochberg corrections would clip out.
